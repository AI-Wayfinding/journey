# Stage 4: sensemaking rounds

## Summary <!-- work:summary -->
Deliver recurring sensemaking rounds in a journey or one project, with purpose, versioned interview guidance, contribution instructions, related artifacts, participants, facilitators and planning/gathering/making sense/complete states. Support skip, reverse and reopen. Deliver private-by-default interviews with verified facilitator labels, unchanged authorship and the interview subject's read-only exception; private interviews and subject deliveries use Stage 3's fixed-slot server-held member vault. Save shared findings only with durable, exact member confirmation and current content-write authority. Deliver browser and CLI/MCP workflows and safe existing-link round views. Preserve Stage 0–3 controls, projects, private existence secrecy, attachments and viewers. This is an implementation plan, not implementation approval or passing evidence.

## Why <!-- work:rationale -->
Ontology section 9 Stage 4, sections 2–7's round/interview/contribution relationships and access rows, and decision 0001 D5–D7, D9–D11, D16–D26, D28, D30–D31, D33–D38 and D44 define the outcome. Amendments override earlier wording: a facilitator who is not the author cannot read a private interview merely by conducting or labelling it; D44 adds the verified subject's read access, not edits or a general recipient grant. Round control is separate from content authority. Contributor, confirmer, immutable artifact author, interview subject and actual version writer are separate identities. All stages remain in MVP scope, but this graph delivers Stage 4 only. Ontology approval and completed Stage 3 integration remain external implementation prerequisites; writing or promoting this draft grants neither.

Binding operator direction overrides conflicting historical source recommendations: agents get exactly their affiliated member's current access, with no original role cap, person-only round operation or other special agent restriction. They sign as their approved agent identity, never with a person's key or an independent membership. Private journeys admit only by invitation or approval. Stage 4 does not activate public journeys or change admission. Stage 3's final Operator/Technical-lead decisions and .work/specs/stage-3-private-artifacts.yaml override its earlier variable-bucket, whole-blob, save-triggered sync and unconditional fork-refusal wording: one member vault, 64 × 1 MiB ciphertext slots allocated at join, fixed open/5-minute two-slot sync, signed headers/checkpoints and paired/unpaired freshness with verified per-artifact fork merging. These are settled dependencies, not Questions for Dan.

All round/participant/facilitator authority, subject audience, confirmation eligibility, lifecycle, revision, reference and version decisions belong in production Bend functions in packages/rules, stated in LAWS.bend and proved for those actual functions in PROOF.bend. TypeScript verifies exact schemas, signatures, hashes, key/admission/journey/epoch bindings and byte counts; copies named fields; encrypts, stores and transports; and projects verified Bend results. Extend scripts/check-bend-boundary.mjs without weakening prior stages. Public ControlProof is the only journey/round authority; private relationship history stays inside Stage 3 encryption and cannot become a competing membership authority. Read the Bend guide through scripts/bend.mjs before authoring laws/code; preserve approved earlier law meanings.

A hostile or careless caller must not create a round without content-write authority; use guide/facilitator/participant/confirmation status to gain artifact or private access; assign foreign, removed or expired members; revive grants through re-admission; obtain independent agent access; relabel an interview to change its author; disclose a private interview's existence through shared round links, proofs, counts, blobs, exports, errors or logs; give a subject edits/comments/copy grants or reveal it to unrelated facilitators, members, public readers or links; replay a revoked relationship or stale confirmation; save unconfirmed findings through an ordinary artifact-version route; substitute a contributor, writer, document, bytes or base revision after confirmation; overwrite concurrent edits; resurrect deleted content; bypass pending rotation for content; smuggle extra caller fields; or obtain partial content with missing capabilities. Previously received copies/keys cannot be recalled. Opaque vault storage does not give the server private-artifact authority, and fixed ciphertext alone does not prove traffic secrecy.

Out of scope for this stage: framework additions or a new interviewing methodology; collaborative real-time cursors/CRDT editing; private-to-shared interview conversion; arbitrary recipient sharing or an automatic private relay; public journey/listing/joining/conversion/shared-copy activation (Stage 6); HTML/applet runtime (Stage 5); general shared-read refactoring/full client-link parity and drops (Stage 7); account recovery/key-persistence redesign and unrelated hardening. Preserve header/footer, approval-first agent connection, encryption, Stage 1 byte/viewer limits and Stage 2 main/project/all selection. No deployment, provisioning, package publication, real Keychain/journey access or remote action is evidence.

## Decisions taken

These technical-lead choices resolve the build questions without reopening settled product rules. Review them against the final shipped prerequisites before implementation; unresolved product policy is recorded separately below.

1. **Round identity, home and document:** use a journey-scoped ULID `round`, with exactly one immutable home `{kind:'journey'}` or `{kind:'project',project:<ULID>}` and one immutable shared `sensemaking-document` artifact ID. The project must exist in this journey; project participation/archive does not restrict journey reads or confer round authority. Each round begins `planning`, with trimmed nonempty plain-text purpose of at most 10,000 UTF-8 bytes. Guidance is exactly `{script,cribNotes,guidance}` with plain-text/Markdown strings; instructions are a Markdown string; related artifacts are a unique array of existing, undeleted same-journey shared artifact IDs. Empty guidance/instructions/related lists are valid during planning. The complete decoded round payload stays within 1,048,576 bytes. Creation atomically commits `round.create` followed by the normal empty sensemaking `artifact.create`, naming its immutable actual creator/author. Project-home document placement uses a separate ordinary Stage 2 action after successful creation, not a new placement or atomic-network promise. Round home and artifact placement remain distinct. No round delete or visibility toggle is invented; `complete` preserves history and reopening. Deleting its document does not delete the round or silently recreate the document; reject further contributions and show the unavailable document with round history intact.
2. **One signed shared format:** add exact `format:'round-v1'` actions `round.create`, `round.content`, `round.facilitators`, `round.state`, `round.interview` and `round.contribute` to ControlProof/controlDefinitions. Every body has `format,round`. Create adds `home,document,participants,facilitators,contentHash,state:'planning'`; content adds `contentHash,predecessor`; facilitators adds `facilitators,predecessor`; state adds `state,predecessor`; interview adds `artifact,subject,predecessor,operation`; contribute adds `document,version,predecessor,metadataRevision,artifactProofHash,payloadHash,confirmation`. `participants` is exactly `{mode:'all'}` or `{mode:'subset',members:[{principal,admissionHash}]}`. Member references in subset/facilitator lists bind the creation/current verified admission, not names or email; carry exact `{principal,admissionHash}` entries. Other IDs are ULIDs, hashes use core's canonical SHA-256 representation and revisions are safe nonnegative proof sequences preceding the current proof. Common proof actor is the real signer, with the existing chain, signature, envelopeHash and epoch bindings. Use exact encrypted records `{type:'round.content',typeVersion:1,body:{purpose,interviewGuidance,contributionInstructions,relatedArtifacts}}` for create/content and `{type:'round.marker',typeVersion:1,body:{}}` otherwise. Public actions expose commitments, IDs and control facts, not text; ciphertext is display content, never a second control/grant. Validate exact fields before replay and after decrypt, copying named fields in every adapter/archive path. Unknown actions/payloads fail before partial round output. Shared contentHash is a commitment, not resistance to guessing short text.
3. **Participants and agents:** default all means the live journey membership at creation, retained as verified admission bindings; a chosen subset selects current members at creation. Freeze that admission set, with agents following their affiliated member's inclusion and current access; adding an approved agent later includes it if its member participates. Include an explicitly selected agent through its member's binding rather than inventing independent participation. Removing/expiry of a person or agent removes its effective participation; a fresh admission never revives the old binding. New people are not silently enrolled into earlier rounds. Project-home creation still defaults to all journey members, not only project participants. Participation organizes the work: nonparticipants keep ordinary shared reads, and it grants no private read or ordinary write. No join/leave/roster editor is added pending Q1. Agent reads and authorized controls/writes equal the current member's access; superseded original read-only caps/person-only operation bans must not survive as tests.
4. **Facilitators and round controls:** creation requires a current read-write member and at least one live same-journey facilitator binding. Assignment is not guide authority and may identify a person or an approved agent acting through its member's assignment; represent the underlying member assignment once, derive effective agents without extra grants. Assigned facilitators of either content role may change state and mark/unmark interview relationships. State values are exactly `planning`, `gathering`, `making-sense`, `complete`, shown with spaces. Every different-state transition is allowed, including direct completion, reverse, skipping and reopening to any noncomplete state. No-op/stale transitions conflict; record signer/time/old/new state/revision. Round state uses its own revision; content and facilitator sets each use independent revisions, initially the creation sequence. State is a workflow label, not a write/read freeze, so authorized work remains possible after complete. Shared endpoints return malformed 400, access denial 403, stale/no-op revision 409, oversize 413 and unsupported client 426 without effects; hidden private targets remain uniformly unavailable. Creator/guide status alone cannot move state, mark an interview or read it. Removal/expiry invalidates assignments immediately; role downgrade removes content writes but not facilitator controls. Pending rotation blocks content/document writes, not authorized facilitator state/relationship revocation controls. Assignment replacement policy is Q1; define its strict schema now but do not activate an unapproved reassignment rule.
5. **Versioned round content and references:** purpose/guidance/instructions/related links share one observed content revision; a current read-write journey member edits via `round.content`, never D17 project-metadata permission or facilitator status alone. A concurrent stale revision returns 409 with refresh; retain the unsaved draft rather than silently merging or winning. History exposes each revision's actual writer and signed time. Related shared references never carry a private copy ID, subject, title, placeholder or count. Private related interviews are a viewer-local overlay rebuilt from that viewer's verified vault relationship history; absence of private material changes no shared row/count/cursor. At projection time omit deleted, foreign and audience-inaccessible targets before counts/links, not by rendering placeholders. Guidance/instructions edits do not rewrite earlier confirmations: new contributions bind the then-current metadataRevision. An intervening edit invalidates an uncommitted proposal, requiring renewed review. Marking an existing `document` or `data` as an interview is a relationship, not a type mutation; activate `interview` and `sensemaking-document` types with exact `{kind,markdown}` content using Stage 1 attachment/payload limits. This lets later Stage 5 data reads compose with subject-readable `data` relationships without inventing an interview parser. Legacy interview/position tags do not establish relationships or grants.
6. **Private interview authority stays hidden:** extend encrypted Stage 3 PrivateRecord history with `private.interview` actions, not a public `interview.mark` action or a `recipients` field. A private copy has zero-or-one active `{round,subject}` relationship; marking, replacement and unmarking consume its observed relationship predecessor and preserve immutable author/type/copy identity and content head. Subject is one current member admission in the containing journey; participation is not a condition for this read exception. round/facilitator evidence comes from a complete verified ControlProof snapshot. An exact facilitator-signed certificate is `{format:'round-v1',purpose:'interview-relationship',journey,round,copy,artifact,author,subject,predecessor,operation,authorityHead,actor,at,sig}` with `operation:'mark'|'unmark'|'revoke'`, subject null only for unmark and revoke retaining the previously bound subject, and author/subject using Stage 3 pinned kind/signingKey/recipient plus journey admission binding. It remains encrypted inside the author's vault. The accepting client verifies the real signer and current assignment using Bend; knowing an ID, supplying ciphertext labels, conducting an interview or a copied certificate does not suffice. A nonauthor facilitator can sign an opaque request supplied locally by the author without seeing interview content; the author imports/accepts it into the vault before any subject key delivery. Author acceptance is the verified vault commit/key-delivery step for this exact D44 relationship, not a separate consent policy or arbitrary-sharing grant. It cannot substitute for the required facilitator signature or turn facilitator status alone into read access. The author may always terminate future subject delivery; record `operation:'revoke'` signed by the verified author with the same exact identity/predecessor/authority bindings, never by the subject or an unrelated facilitator. Subject access requires both an accepted facilitator relationship and live author/subject admissions, with author-revocation taking precedence. PrivateRecord actor remains the actual vault-record writer; its nested certificate actor is the actual facilitator or revoking author, whose admitted key signs sig. Author acceptance binds the certificate hash, copy and current relationship head in the normal signed private record. Mark/unmark/accept/revoke are narrow relationship/key-delivery controls: an otherwise authorized read-only facilitator or author may perform them, including during rotation, but cannot alter interview content, comments, attachments or placement. This is not a blanket Stage 3 private-write exception. A facilitator who has neither author nor subject authority never decrypts the interview merely to label it. For a shared interview, use `round.interview` ControlProof body `{format:'round-v1',round,artifact,subject,predecessor,operation}`, with operation mark/unmark and subject a verified `{principal,admissionHash}` or null only for unmark. Bind its independent relationship predecessor, derive immutable author from verified artifact history and use the common proof actor/signature. No private copy/key/author-certificate fields enter this variant. It is allowed only for an existing shared artifact and valid facilitator. It changes neither the normal shared audience nor ordinary artifact writes. Private IDs/certificates never use this public variant.
7. **Subject delivery, freshness and revocation:** D44 adds only the verified subject's read audience, including its approved agents acting with exactly that member's access; no other facilitator, person, agent, link, recovery recipient or public viewer gains a key. Subject-not-author is read-only even when read-write in the journey: no version/comment/delete/project/copy/handoff/relationship mutation. Author and actual writers retain Stage 3 author-audience rules; subject marking never transfers authorship. Reuse Stage 3 explicit local encrypted handoff and dual-key possession challenge, specialized to a verified relationship: deliver only this copy's eligible snapshot/history/attachment keys, facilitator certificate, author acceptance/current relationship head and supporting authority histories, age-encrypted to the verified subject identity. Add exact bundle scope `interview-subject`; no author root/index key, arbitrary recipient, remote URL or shared wrap/approval attachment. A subject stores received encrypted snapshots/checkpoints in its own existing padded vault; it never fetches/writes another member's vault. Its approved agents use the same scoped encrypted delivery mechanism. Subject delivery includes only related-copy material, not the author's other private entries. Author/imported agent updates and label changes queue normally for fixed-schedule vault commit; never trigger an extra fetch/upload or enlarge slots. Author stages a handoff only from a verified committed relationship/content head. Subject import similarly becomes pending until its scheduled commit; full-vault refusal is atomic and cannot grant an incomplete view. A read-only member may persist verified received read snapshots/checkpoints in its own encrypted vault/cache, including scheduled transport and dummy-slot updates; this is possession/cache maintenance, never authority to author, edit or return the interview. Distinguish received-snapshot records from author-owned PrivateRecords so generic create/edit/copy/return cannot use this cache exception.
8. **Verified audience adapter for later stages:** expose one core `roundAudience` adapter using verified Stage 3 copy/identity, round/admission, relationship head and viewer credential facts. Return shared, author-private, interview-subject-read-only or unavailable plus permitted read/write operations; caller flags, contributor confirmation, guide/project/facilitator status cannot manufacture an audience. Recheck current ordinary authority and relationship validity after async crypto and before render/download/export/applet delivery. Online subject refresh is explicit local challenge/response with the author: fresh one-use challenge and subject/copy/session binding, signed author current relationship head and selected content head, signature-verified against the retained relationship checkpoint. The author rechecks live round/admission/revocation authority before answering; accepting code consumes the challenge and rechecks authority. No response, known-stale relationship, revoked/replaced label, subject removal/expiry/re-admission or deleted copy yields unavailable, not empty/fallback/shared content. A previous bundle is already-delivered cached material, not evidence of current online permission. Permit explicitly labelled offline previously received reads with freshness unconfirmed under Stage 3; do not pass those snapshots as current authority to Stage 5's live broker. There is no automatic polling of another author's vault, private server permission table or immediate remote recall promise. Upon a verified revoke/replace/remove/delete notice, clear active subject-only views/keys and cancel pending deliveries; downloaded offline bytes remain outside recall. Unmark/relabel rotates future copy-content keys and excludes the former subject from new bundles, preserving old author history encrypted under its vault root. Historical keys already handed over cannot be made secret again. Round completion/archive does not revoke a valid relationship; facilitator reassignment does not invalidate an already accepted label by itself. Cross-journey copies never carry a round/subject grant: destination relationship starts absent and needs a fresh same-journey mark. Stage 5 consumes this exact verified result/recheck, Stage 6 omits private relationships/public rosters and copies only already-shared findings, and Stage 7 excludes every private interview from link reads regardless of author/subject. Add explicit unavailable/stale and before/after revocation fixtures for those consumers, not substitute audience stubs that bypass private storage.
9. **Exact contribution and confirmation:** a contribution is an action producing a new version of the round's shared document, not another artifact type or a transcript publication. Prepare a full proposed Markdown document version and an explicit findings/diff preview from the observed current document head and current instructions revision. Confirmation is exactly `{format:'round-v1',purpose:'contribution-confirmation',id,journey,round,document,contributor,confirmer,writer,version,predecessor,metadataRevision,artifactProofHash,payloadHash,at,sig}`. IDs are fresh ULIDs, `predecessor` is the observed document version, contributor/confirmer/writer are verified admitted principals, and the actual confirmer signs canonical JSON omitting only sig. Contributor is a live journey member; round participation organizes work and is recorded, not an extra ordinary content permission. Its member or approved agent acting through that member's binding may explicitly confirm with equal access, signing as itself and retaining that actual confirmer identity; an unrelated facilitator/guide/writer cannot confirm for it. An agent contributor resolves through its approved member binding, not a fabricated self-affiliation. No blanket approval, inferred confirmation from chat/tool execution, unsigned checkbox, reused confirmation ID or author-written claim is accepted. CLI/MCP confirmation is a distinct explicit signed operation over the exact preview/package, not an automatic side effect of prepare/save. Read-only participants may confirm but cannot save. The UI states that confirmed findings, not the private interview, will become journey-shared (and later public in a public journey); it does not infer that the subject consented merely because the interview is readable.
10. **Saving and concurrency:** writer prepares/signs the normal artifact.version proof and exact ciphertext/payload/attachments for the proposed complete version before confirmation; confirmation binds that proof hash, plaintext payload hash, writer, document/version/base and metadata revision. Confirming client verifies signature, decrypts/previews the final package and checks complete payload/live attachments before signing. The server verifies proof/confirmation binding, not equality of different encrypted plaintexts; keyed readers verify the payload hash. `/rounds/:round/contributions` accepts the prepared normal artifact.version and following `round.contribute` as one atomic two-proof operation. The latter embeds the exact confirmation and binds the preceding artifact proof hash. Current Bend replay requires this pair for every version of a designated round document; standalone `/log`/generic artifact writes, blank initial content with preloaded findings, a forged relationship/type or importing an orphan version cannot bypass it. Creation similarly accepts only the paired round.create/empty artifact.create; replay rejects incomplete pairs before projecting any view. Any actual saving member needs current content-write authority, current epoch/capabilities and no pending rotation; confirmation adds no permission. Check current contributor/confirmer agency/admission, current writer, metadata/document predecessors, unused confirmation and undeleted document after upload/crypto and inside serialized commit. Exact committed retries are idempotent under current access gates; changed retries, consumed IDs, stale base/instructions or role/removal/expiry races fail without either half committing. Any changed proof chain position, epoch, ciphertext or writer requires preparation and confirmation again; unrelated controls may therefore conflict too. Do not silently re-sign a changed package after confirmation. Every accepted version retains immutable document author, actual writer, contributor, actual confirmer, round, confirmation signature/hash and sequence/time. Use whole-version optimistic concurrency, not CRDT or last-write-wins: after conflict reread/rebase locally, prepare again and obtain new confirmation. Generic title/body edits to a designated document also use this path; ordinary comments/deletion retain D5, and comments are not additions to its findings. An undeleted sensemaking snapshot copied by later Stage 6 has no destination round relationship; it is not a contribution to the source round or evidence of destination confirmation. No private copy ID, subject label, transcript, certificate, root/copy key or private-related reference is automatically inserted into shared findings or attribution.
